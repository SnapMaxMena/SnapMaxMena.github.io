Edition × compliance — the exam ladder
Standard
SOC 1 Type II · SOC 2 Type II · ISO 27001/17/18 · ISO 9001 · CSA STAR L1 — basic audit/security posture only
Enterprise
+ PCI-DSS · HITRUST CSF · 90-day Time Travel · Multi-cluster WH · Data Classification (PII tagging)
Business Critical
+ HIPAA / HITRUST BAA · FedRAMP (Moderate & High) · ITAR (Gov regions) · Tri-Secret Secure · Private Link · Customer-managed keys · Account failover/failback
VPS
+ Fully isolated single-tenant Snowflake on dedicated AWS VPC. Maximum isolation. Includes all Business Critical features.
⚠ HIPAA, FedRAMP, and ITAR require Business Critical edition or higher. A signed BAA (Business Associate Agreement) must exist before storing PHI.

Shared responsibility model — exam trap
Snowflake's responsibility
Maintain certifications · Secure the platform infrastructure · Provide encryption, audit logging, key management, network isolation
Customer's responsibility
Configure RBAC correctly · Sign BAA (HIPAA) · Enable MFA · Choose correct edition · Monitor access · Manage their data
⚠ Having Business Critical edition does NOT automatically make you HIPAA-compliant — a BAA must be signed AND the environment properly configured.
Certification one-liners — must memorize
SOC 2 Type II
Most cited. Audited security, availability, confidentiality. All editions.
HIPAA
Protected Health Information (PHI). Business Critical+ + signed BAA required.
FedRAMP
US federal cloud risk management. AWS + Azure only. Business Critical+.
ITAR
US military export controls. Gov regions only. Business Critical+.
PCI-DSS
Payment card security. Attestation of Compliance by Qualified Security Assessor.
HITRUST CSF
Healthcare security framework (HIPAA + HITECH + state law). Business Critical+.
ISO 27001
ISMS standard. Globally recognized. All editions.
BAA
Business Associate Agreement. Legal contract. Required before any PHI goes into Snowflake.
